Fix the bare-metal build: f64::round() doesn't exist in no_std

period_finding_ecdlp.rs called the std-only f64::round() method, which
doesn't exist without std and broke the real x86_64-unknown-none release
and debug builds outright (the hosted feature build masked it, since it
links against a full std). Every other float call in this crate goes
through libm's free functions; switched this one to match. Verified
clean across all three: bare-metal release, bare-metal debug, and the
hosted build, zero warnings on all three.
