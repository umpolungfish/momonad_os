Serve the word instruments automatically after imasm write and imasm derive

Chasing the efl_affirm bank leak by hand this session meant running weight,
banked, cycle and insert as four separate commands after imasm derive handed
back a word. That is exactly backwards: those four questions apply to any
word a tool outputs, not only the ones I happen to go looking at by hand.

imasm write and imasm derive now run all four automatically on the word they
just produced and print the result under the tuple and crystal address,
instead of leaving it to whoever reads the word to remember to ask. Nothing
here gates on the answer, closing, open, banked or not, still prints; the
point is that the information is served, not that anything blocks on it.

Built and confirmed against a live word with a real banked-count defect: the
derive output now shows the movement, the exposed clear, the ROTAT orbit and
the five one-glyph repairs in one call, where it used to show only the word,
the tuple and the crystal address.
