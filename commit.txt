I added a release workflow that builds distributable binaries for Linux, Windows, and macOS natively on GitHub's own runners, rather than cross-compiling from here: real MSVC on windows-latest, real Xcode on macos-latest, no SDK extraction or mingw/Wine tricks anywhere.

The macOS job builds both Apple architectures and merges them into one universal binary with lipo, since a single-arch guess would leave out either the Intel or the Apple Silicon half of actual Mac users. Every job checks out Vox and MoDoT as siblings of mOMonadOS at the branches this crate currently builds against locally, since Cargo.toml resolves them by relative path and the runner's checkout has to match that layout.

This only takes effect once I push mOMonadOS, Vox, and MoDoT to their remotes — a tag push matching v* then cuts a release with all three binaries attached, and a manual run from the Actions tab just produces build artifacts. Documented in the README under a new Distributable Binaries section.
